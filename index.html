What does the term mean?

A Rizzitgo spreadsheet is a product-discovery list or catalog used to organize shopping references by category. It may include source links, images and sizing notes. A listing is a starting point for research, not a guarantee of stock, quality or authenticity.

01 / Beginner shopping guide

From Rizzitgo finds to a useful shortlist

Learning how to use a Rizzitgo spreadsheet starts with three checks you can repeat for any category.

Keep the source attached

Save the source product URL, seller name and product identifier together. For Taobao product links, Weidian listings or 1688 product references, check the original page rather than relying on a copied thumbnail. Record the selected color and size so your product variant check stays specific.

Compare measurements first

A useful Rizzitgo sizing guide starts with the exact listing's clothing size chart. Use chest width measurement for tops, jacket sleeve length for outerwear and pants inseam for trousers. For shoes, compare foot length measurement with the model's guidance; a shoe size conversion alone may miss fit differences.

Separate photos from proof

Read Rizzitgo QC photos alongside the listing and selected variant. Warehouse inspection photos may reveal uneven stitching, an unexpected shade or a missing component. Note QC photo limitations: camera light changes color, and images cannot establish material composition or how an item will wear.

04 / Reading room

Two notes to read before ordering

Keep uncertainty visible in your notes. A missing detail is a reason to investigate further.

Field note / Product links

When a link changes, pause the comparison

Rizzitgo product links are only useful while they still identify the item you researched. A listing can disappear, change its available options or display a different main image.

For unavailable product links, search using the exact model or product identifier. Compare seller identity and selected variant again before treating a replacement as equivalent. A similar photograph is not enough.

Keep your original URL, the date you checked it and the unresolved question in one note. If you cannot connect the reference to a current listing, leave it off the purchase shortlist.

Field note / Shipping

Make room for the costs outside the item

A Rizzitgo shipping checklist should separate an international shipping estimate from a final amount due. Destination, packaging, route limits and the chargeable weight can all affect the calculation.

Budget = item + domestic delivery + service charges + international freight + applicable fees

Ask whether actual or volumetric weight applies. Check currency conversion fees, optional services and any destination charges relevant to your parcel. Confirm the current terms with the service provider before paying; this page does not quote freight or delivery dates.

05 / Direct answers

A few questions worth asking.

Clear answers for using a product-discovery list without confusing a reference, a catalog and a checkout.

What is a Rizzitgo spreadsheet?

A Rizzitgo spreadsheet is a product-discovery list or catalog used to organize shopping references by category. It may include source links, images and sizing notes. A listing is a starting point for research, not a guarantee of stock, quality or authenticity.

Is this an official Rizzitgo website or a downloadable sheet?

No. This is an independent reading guide, not an official agent website, live inventory feed or downloadable spreadsheet. Its category links open CAFANS, a separate destination; they do not open Rizzitgo checkout.

How do I check Rizzitgo product links?

Open the original listing and compare its seller, product identifier, selected color and size with your saved reference. Recheck availability and ordering terms. If a link is unavailable, look for the exact model and variant rather than assuming a similar image is the same item.

Do QC photos prove an item is authentic?

No. Warehouse inspection photos can help show visible condition, color, measurements and obvious defects, but they do not establish authenticity, material composition or long-term durability. Confirm provenance separately with the seller.

How should I choose a clothing or shoe size?

Use the exact listing's size chart and measurement method. Compare garment measurements with a well-fitting item you own, and foot length with the model's footwear guidance. Letter sizes and EU, UK or US labels alone are not enough.

What should a shipping estimate include?

Check product cost, domestic delivery, service charges, international freight, currency conversion fees and any applicable destination charges. Ask whether the route uses actual or volumetric weight. A preliminary estimate is not a final quote.
